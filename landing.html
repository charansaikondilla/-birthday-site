What's in the box

Built like a cinema, not a slideshow app.

Every contributor's name scrolls past on a black screen, film-house style. This is the part people screenshot.

Nobody installs anything

Contributors tap the link, pick their photos and videos, and they're finished. Works on any phone, any browser, any country.

A QR code for the party

Print it, prop it on the table, stick it inside a card. Guests film on the spot and it's in the reel before the cake arrives.

Before you ask

The four things everybody wants to know.

Does the birthday person need an account?

No. They open a link and it plays. Same for everyone contributing — a link is the whole experience, on both ends.

What happens after seven days on the free pass?

The link stops working and the files are permanently deleted. Upgrade to Keep Forever any time before that and nothing is lost.

Can I see what people have uploaded first?

Yes. You hold the organiser link, so you can review everything and remove anything before it premieres.

How long does a finished reel run?

Usually two to six minutes, depending on how much comes in. Long enough to feel like a film, short enough to watch twice.
